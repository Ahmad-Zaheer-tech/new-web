Examina

An online exam platform with two modes: timed online exams with instant grading, and print-ready offline exams with answer keys.

Visit live siteAll projects

Two modes

Online exams

Interactive, timed assessments with a live countdown, a question navigator, and instant auto-graded results — the full exam experience in the browser.

Offline exams

The same exam banks as print-ready paper tests, each with a separate answer key — for classrooms without devices or take-home practice.

What it includes

  • Timed online exams with a live countdown, a question navigator, and instant auto-graded results.
  • Offline mode: the same exam banks as print-ready paper tests, each with a separate answer key.
  • Timer, answers, and progress save automatically, so a refresh does not cost the attempt; it works offline once loaded.
  • Filter by subject and level, light and dark mode; answers stay in the browser — nothing is uploaded or tracked.
