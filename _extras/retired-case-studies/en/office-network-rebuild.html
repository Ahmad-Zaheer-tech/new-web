Office Network Rebuild

Full network overhaul for a small office — segmented Wi-Fi, labeled cabling, documented handover.

Networking Hardware

The challenge

A small office's network had grown organically over several years — unlabeled cables, no separation between guest and staff Wi-Fi, and no documentation of what was plugged in where. Every issue took hours to diagnose because nothing was mapped.

The approach

Rebuilt the physical and logical network from the switch layout up: segmented Wi-Fi for guests versus staff, labeled every cable run, and documented the full topology in a one-page reference sheet.

The outcome

Future network issues that used to take hours to trace now take minutes, because the documentation exists and anyone on-site can follow it — not just the person who set it up.
