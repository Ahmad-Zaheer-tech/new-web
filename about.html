Summary

I hold a computer science diploma from the Afghanistan Institute of Technology (AIT) and work at the point where data, software, and teaching meet. I administered the PHEMIS database system at Payam University for a year, and I have taught English and computer courses at five institutions: Abraj, Ganja and Amozesh Educational Centers, Badr Academy, and Andisha-e-Noor Academy.

As a developer I build with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Tailwind CSS, and PHP, and I work with SQL, SQL Server, and Oracle. I created and published two platforms of my own: Ahmad Zaheer Academy, a free programming academy with 11 courses and 450+ lessons, and Examina, an online and offline exam platform.

I also use AI tools, generative AI, and AI agents to speed up building software, producing media, and handling office work.
