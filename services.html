How an engagement works

The same four steps whether the job takes two days or two months.

Discovery

A short conversation (or a written brief) about what you need built, fixed, or taught — and whether I am the right fit.

Scope & plan

A plain-language outline of what will be delivered, in what order, and roughly how long it takes — before either side commits.

Build

Regular check-ins instead of silence until the end, so feedback arrives while it can still change the work.

Handover

Clear documentation of what was built and how to maintain it, plus a defined window of after-launch support.

On cost

Pricing depends on scope, timeline, and whether the work is a one-off build or ongoing support, so it is not listed here. Every quote is given in writing, after the discovery call and before work starts.

On availability

Working remotely in English and Dari, on Kabul time (UTC+4:30). New inquiries are usually answered within 1–2 business days.
